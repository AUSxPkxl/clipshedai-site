Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

ClipShedAI ("we", "us") is an internal tool operated by ClipShedAU in Australia. This policy explains what data ClipShedAI uses and how. Contact: clipshed@pkxl.net.

Who uses ClipShedAI

ClipShedAI is used only by ClipShedAU to manage ClipShedAU's own social media accounts. It is not available to the public, and we do not collect personal information from members of the public through it.

Data we access

  • YouTube (via YouTube API Services): with ClipShedAU's authorisation, we upload videos to ClipShedAU's own YouTube channel and read basic statistics (such as view counts) for videos we uploaded.
  • Instagram (via the Instagram Platform / Meta APIs): with ClipShedAU's authorisation, we publish Reels to ClipShedAU's own Instagram account and read basic insights for those posts.
  • Twitch and other streaming platforms: we read public information about livestreams (such as whether a channel is live and which game is being played) and public stream video, only for creators whose clipping campaigns we have joined.

How data is stored and shared

Access tokens and data are stored on our own computer and are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties, except that caption text (not account data) may be sent to an AI text service to help draft captions. We do not use data for advertising.

Google and YouTube

ClipShedAI uses YouTube API Services. By using ClipShedAI you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies. You can revoke ClipShedAI's access to a Google account at any time from the Google security settings page. ClipShedAI's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data deletion

To have any data held by ClipShedAI deleted, email clipshed@pkxl.net with the subject "Data deletion". We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email. Stored tokens are also deleted when access is revoked from Google or Meta account settings. Data we hold is deleted when it is no longer needed, and access tokens are refreshed or deleted at least every 90 days.

Changes

We will update this page if anything changes, with a new "Last updated" date.
