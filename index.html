ClipShedAI

Livestream highlights, cut for short-form.

ClipShedAI is the in-house tool behind ClipShedAU. It finds the best moments from livestreams we are authorised to clip through official creator clipping campaigns, edits them into vertical short-form videos with captions, and publishes them to ClipShedAU's own YouTube and Instagram accounts.

How we use YouTube

ClipShedAI uses YouTube API Services (the YouTube Data API) to upload approved clips to ClipShedAU's own YouTube channel and to read view counts for those videos. See our Privacy Policy for how YouTube data is handled, and the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Every clip is reviewed and approved by a person before it is posted. ClipShedAI only ever posts to accounts owned by ClipShedAU. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's accounts.
